Create Your Contractor Profile



Set up the business details MyHomeBro uses to organize your work.



Welcome to MyHomeBro. In this quick walkthrough, you will create your contractor account and set up a useful business profile.

Begin by entering your name, an email and phone number you control, and a strong password. Review the Terms and Privacy Policy, then create the account.

Select Get started. Your answers help MyHomeBro prepare editable project and workflow defaults. Nothing is published or sent automatically.

Describe the work you usually perform in plain language. Include your main trades and common project types so the suggested setup fits your business.

Next, review your business type, service area, and operating details. Only claim licenses, insurance, or emergency availability that accurately apply to your business.

Review the suggested workflow, pricing baseline, and project defaults. These are starting points, so confirm they fit your work before selecting Looks good.

Your initial profile is ready. You can return to Profile whenever your services, contact information, or service area changes.
