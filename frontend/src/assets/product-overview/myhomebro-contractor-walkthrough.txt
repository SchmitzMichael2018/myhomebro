From customer request to paid project

Welcome to MyHomeBro for contractors: one connected path from customer request to completed, paid project.

The dashboard brings today's priorities, schedule, active work, opportunities, and payments into one operational view.

New website and homeowner requests arrive in Opportunities. Review the project, qualify the lead, communicate with the customer, and decide the next step.

The estimate queue organizes scope, pricing, customer review, acceptance, and the handoff into a formal agreement.

Agreements keep signed terms, funding, amendments, milestones, and invoices connected. Contractors can quickly distinguish drafts, active work, and completed project history.

Milestones turn the agreement into a practical work plan. Track progress, coordinate completion, collect customer review, document rework when needed, and move approved work toward invoicing.

Team profiles, roles, access, and capabilities support clear assignments while preserving the contractor's review and approval authority.

The money workspace connects escrow funding, invoices, approved releases, paid records, refunds, and resolution holds to the underlying agreement and project.

Payout history provides the audit trail needed to reconcile what moved, when it moved, and which project earned it.

With MyHomeBro, contractors can estimate, coordinate, document, and get paid through one clear, connected workflow.
