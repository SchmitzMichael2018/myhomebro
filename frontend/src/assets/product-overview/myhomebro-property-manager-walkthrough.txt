From maintenance request to property record

A property-focused tour of maintenance intake, vendor coordination, approvals, completion, and history.

Welcome to MyHomeBro property operations, where maintenance stays connected from resident request through lasting property history.

Home brings open actions, active work, recent updates, maintenance recommendations, and the property portfolio into one clear view.

Resident requests arrive with the property, unit, urgency, description, contact details, access permission, and supporting photos or documents.

The manager reviews the facts, requests more information when needed, records notes, and approves valid work before a work order is created.

Approved requests become traceable work orders with a responsible vendor or contractor, schedule, priority, resident context, and activity history.

Completion stays documented with assignment, schedule, notes, evidence, and a full activity timeline, instead of disappearing after the repair is finished.

The property record connects the building profile, units, tenants, open maintenance, systems, and responsible people for each location.

Maintenance Center surfaces due-soon and needs-attention items, so planned upkeep can be addressed before it becomes urgent.

Timeline and history preserve maintenance, documents, warranties, photos, projects, and completed service for future planning and informed decisions.

MyHomeBro helps property teams review, coordinate, complete, and preserve every maintenance record in one connected workflow.
