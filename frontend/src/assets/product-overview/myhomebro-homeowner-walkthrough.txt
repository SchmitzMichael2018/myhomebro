MyHomeBro Homeowner Walkthrough

This walkthrough uses AI-generated narration.

Welcome to MyHomeBro, where every part of your home project stays connected in one clear, secure workspace.

Your Home screen highlights what needs attention, active projects, recent updates, and helpful property recommendations.

When you are ready to start something new, describe the work in your own words. Your request stays private until you choose to share it with a contractor.

As estimates arrive, compare scope, pricing, project details, and the accepted version in one organized place.

Once work begins, the project workspace connects your agreement, milestones, documents, warranties, and next steps, so everyone understands what comes next.

The Payment Center clearly shows what was funded, released, refunded, or remains in escrow. Every payment stays connected to its project and supporting record.

Property Records preserve project history, documents, warranties, photos, maintenance information, and home-system details for future reference.

Updates gives you one organized place to follow recent project activity, review important changes, and find anything that may need your attention.

With MyHomeBro, you can plan, review, communicate, and preserve your complete home-project record, from the first request through the final result.
