# MyHomeBro Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: September 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how MyHomeBro LLC ("MyHomeBro," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you use MyHomeBro websites, applications, communications, public profiles, contractor websites, and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). It also explains the choices and rights available to you.

## 1. Scope and Our Role

This Policy applies to homeowners, customers, contractors, property managers, residents, vendors, project team members, website visitors, and other Platform users.

MyHomeBro generally determines how personal data is used to operate the Platform. In some property-management or business workflows, a contractor or property manager may independently control information it enters about residents, employees, properties, or customers. Those businesses are responsible for their own notices and legal obligations. Third-party services, including Stripe, also process data under their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

- **Account and contact data:** name, email address, phone number, login and security information, communication preferences, role, and account status.
- **Business and verification data:** company name, service categories, service areas, licenses, insurance details, tax or business identifiers, authorized representatives, public profile, and verification results.
- **Property and project data:** service addresses, property and unit details, project requests, measurements, scope, schedules, budgets, estimates, agreements, amendments, milestones, work orders, assignments, maintenance requests, warranties, and completion records.
- **Resident and team data:** resident, employee, subcontractor, vendor, or property-management contact information and the access, assignment, or invitation relationship supplied by an authorized user.
- **Documents and content:** signatures, timestamps, IP records, messages, notes, photos, video, receipts, invoices, expense records, portfolio content, reviews, dispute evidence, inspection reports, and uploaded third-party documents.
- **Payment data:** payment-provider customer, account, charge, transfer, refund, payout, and bank or card identifiers; limited payment details such as brand and last four digits; amounts, status, fees, and transaction history. We do not ordinarily receive or store full card numbers or online-banking credentials entered directly into Stripe.
- **Communications data:** email, text-message, call, push, and in-app delivery status; consent and opt-out records; support requests; and responses to reminders and notifications.
- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser, device type, operating system, identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, pages and features used, clicks, timestamps, referring pages, crash information, and security events.
- **Public, directory, and lead-source data:** public business listings, service and location information, Google Places or similar directory data, referring site, first landing page, referral source, bounded campaign parameters (such as UTM source, medium, campaign, content, and term), and search or marketplace interaction data.
- **First-party attribution data:** a random visitor token, first and meaningful last marketing touch, role selections, and basic account, project, and conversion events. We do not use browser fingerprinting for attribution, and we do not collect precise location for attribution.
- **AI interaction data:** prompts, project context, files, selections, suggested content, feedback, and AI-generated output when you use Project Assistant or an AI-enabled feature.

Please do not provide sensitive personal data that a feature does not request. Government identification, precise location, license records, financial information, and information about a person's home or occupancy may require additional care and are used only for the relevant feature, security, compliance, or support purpose.

## 3. Where Information Comes From

We collect information:

- directly from you;
- from another authorized user, such as a homeowner, contractor, property manager, resident, team administrator, or person who invites or assigns you;
- automatically from your browser, device, cookies, and Platform activity;
- from payment, communications, identity, hosting, analytics, mapping, directory, and security providers; and
- from public sources and business directories, including Google Places, where used for contractor discovery, matching, or profile setup.

If you provide information about another person, you represent that you are authorized to do so and will provide any notice or obtain any consent required by law.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use personal data to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and support accounts;
- route project and maintenance requests, match users, and manage opportunities;
- prepare and manage estimates, agreements, amendments, signatures, schedules, work records, warranties, and property history;
- process and reconcile funding, direct payments, draws, expenses, Platform fees, transfers, refunds, and disputes;
- send requested or necessary service communications and maintain consent, delivery, and opt-out records;
- operate public profiles, contractor websites, portfolios, reviews, and lead attribution at the user's direction;
- provide Project Assistant, classification, drafting, summarization, search, and other AI-supported features;
- detect fraud, abuse, duplicate claims, unsafe activity, unauthorized access, and violations of our Terms;
- provide support, troubleshoot, analyze performance, measure campaign and referral effectiveness, understand basic conversion paths, prevent fraud, maintain backups, and improve the service and its usability;
- comply with law, enforce agreements, establish or defend legal claims, and respond to lawful requests; and
- send marketing only where permitted and consistent with your choices.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to perform a contract, pursue legitimate interests in operating and securing the Platform, comply with law, protect vital interests, or based on consent. You may withdraw consent prospectively where processing relies on consent.

## 5. Payments and Stripe

Stripe provides payment processing and connected-account services. Payment credentials and identity or banking information may be collected directly by Stripe. MyHomeBro receives tokens, identifiers, account status, transaction details, and limited payment information needed to display, authorize, reconcile, refund, transfer, prevent fraud, and support transactions.

Stripe processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and legal terms. Do not send full payment-card or bank-login information through Platform messages or uploads.

## 6. Project Assistant and AI Providers

When you use an AI-enabled feature, we may send the prompt and the minimum reasonably necessary account, project, document, image, or workflow context to an AI service provider so it can return a result. Provider output is treated as untrusted until the Platform and user review it.

AI may help draft, classify, summarize, or recommend; it does not make final legal, financial, safety, hiring, payment, refund, or dispute decisions. Users must review output before applying it. We use access controls and contractual or technical safeguards appropriate to the feature, but you should not include secrets or unrelated sensitive data in a prompt.

## 7. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal data:

- **To other Platform users:** as needed for the project, property, invitation, assignment, payment, warranty, refund, or dispute relationship. A contractor may see a customer's request and contact details; a property manager may see resident maintenance information; and authorized team members may see assigned work.
- **Publicly at your direction:** when you publish a contractor profile, website, review, portfolio item, service area, or other public content. Do not publish customer or resident information without permission.
- **To service providers:** including payment processing, communications, hosting, storage, email, security, mapping, directory, analytics, customer support, document, and AI providers that process data for Platform operations.
- **For legal and safety reasons:** to comply with law or lawful process; protect rights, safety, property, and Platform integrity; investigate fraud or abuse; or establish and defend legal claims.
- **In a business transaction:** in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.
- **With consent or direction:** when you ask us to share information or authorize an integration.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not currently use personal data for targeted advertising across unrelated websites. Some privacy laws define "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" broadly; if our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide required choices before using data that way.

Mobile phone opt-in data and text-message consent are not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing. They may be disclosed to communications providers and other parties necessary to deliver messages, support the service, or comply with law.

## 8. Reviews, Public Profiles, and Third-Party Data

Published business names, service areas, websites, portfolio work, ratings, and reviews may be visible to anyone and indexed by search engines. Reviewers and contractors control the content they submit, subject to moderation and our Terms.

If Google or another source provides business or review information, we identify the source where practical and omit ratings when reliable source data is unavailable. Third-party information remains subject to that provider's terms. Contact us if public profile information is inaccurate or you believe it was used without authorization.

## 9. Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

We and our providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, secure the Platform, measure performance, understand feature use, and support communications or lead attribution. MyHomeBro's current campaign and referral attribution is first-party: it records the referring site, landing page, campaign parameters, a random visitor token, and basic conversion events. It does not use browser fingerprinting or precise location. Browser settings can limit some technologies, but blocking essential storage may prevent account or payment features from working.

Where required, we will request consent for nonessential technologies. We do not respond to all browser "Do Not Track" signals because there is no uniform standard, but we honor legally required opt-out mechanisms where applicable.

## 10. Communications Choices

You may change available notification settings in the Platform. You can unsubscribe from marketing email through the message link. For texts, reply **STOP** to opt out, **START** to resume, or **HELP** for help. Transactional message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply.

Opting out of one channel does not prevent essential account, security, legal, or transaction notices through another channel. We retain consent and opt-out records as needed to honor your choice and demonstrate compliance.

## 11. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including providing the Platform, completing transactions, enforcing agreements, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, maintaining property or warranty history, and meeting legal, tax, accounting, and audit obligations.

Retention varies by record. Account and operational data is generally kept while an account or active relationship exists and for a reasonable period afterward. Signed agreements, payment and refund records, disputes, and related audit history may typically be retained for up to seven years or longer where law, an active claim, or a provider obligation requires it. Security logs and support records are retained based on risk and operational need. Backups are protected and age out on a defined cycle.

Deletion or account closure may not remove information another party needs for its lawful project, property, contract, payment, warranty, or dispute record. We may de-identify information so it can no longer reasonably identify you and retain the de-identified information.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have the right to:

- confirm whether we process your personal data and access it;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- delete personal data;
- obtain a portable copy of personal data you provided;
- opt out of a sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling if those practices apply;
- withdraw consent for future processing;
- appeal our decision on a privacy request; and
- receive equal service without unlawful discrimination for exercising a privacy right.

Submit a request or appeal to **info@myhomebro.com** with the subject "Privacy Request." Describe the account, right, and information involved. We will verify your identity and authority using information reasonably related to the request. An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted, but we may require proof of authority and direct identity verification.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any denial and available appeal method. We may retain a request record. If we cannot verify a request, if an exception applies, or if deletion would impair another person's legal record, we may limit or deny the request as permitted by law.

## 13. Security and Incident Response

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, authentication, encryption in transit, provider security controls, logging, backups, and restricted access to sensitive workflows. No system is perfectly secure. Use a unique password, protect your devices, and notify us promptly of suspected unauthorized access.

If a security incident requires notice, we will provide notice to affected people and authorities as required by law.

## 14. Children and Resident Requests

The Platform is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly allow children to create ordinary Platform accounts. A resident maintenance request should be submitted by an adult resident or another authorized person. If you believe a child provided personal data without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 15. U.S. Processing and International Users

MyHomeBro is based in the United States, and information may be processed and stored in the United States and other locations where our providers operate. Those locations may have different privacy laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 16. Third-Party Sites and Services

The Platform may link to contractor websites, Amazon or other retailers, maps, payment services, calendars, social networks, or other third-party services. Their privacy practices and content are controlled by them, not MyHomeBro. Review their policies before providing information. A link or integration is not an endorsement.

## 17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Platform, providers, or law changes. We will update the effective date and provide reasonable notice of material changes when required. We will request consent before materially different processing where law requires consent.
