# Website proposal terms and staff workflow

## Draft service terms

Copy the approved wording into the project’s **Service terms** before sending a proposal. Fill in every bracketed item for the specific project.

MyHomeBro will provide the pages, features, and handoff listed in the proposal at the price shown. The template you selected is a starting point. We’ll customize the design using the brand, content, and features listed in your proposal.

Work is scheduled after payment is confirmed and we receive [required materials and access]. We expect to share a preview [agreed timeframe or target date]. Please provide one consolidated set of feedback per round through Questions & change requests. Your proposal includes [number] rounds of revisions. If we need to change the schedule or scope, we’ll explain the impact and confirm any added work and price with you before proceeding.

You can approve the finished design in your workspace. MyHomeBro will coordinate publication with you after approval. At launch, we’ll provide [files, login access, or other handoff items]. The domain is registered by [party] and paid for by [party]. Hosting is provided by [party] at [cost and billing frequency]. The proposal includes [care or support commitments and duration]. Any separate subscriptions or third-party services listed in the proposal are billed as specified there.

Provide materials you have permission to use and confirm that business details are accurate before approval. If we need account access, we’ll coordinate the method through the workspace.

## Draft cancellation and refund terms

Copy the approved wording into **Refund terms**. Confirm the amounts and deadlines with the business owner before sending a customer proposal.

To request cancellation or a refund, open your website project and send a message under **Questions & change requests**. Include the project name and why you’re requesting cancellation or a refund. We’ll acknowledge the request within [timeframe] and confirm when work will stop and what refund applies under your proposal.

- Before work begins, the refund is [amount or calculation]. Any approved third-party cost deducted from the refund is listed here [items and amounts, or none].
- After work begins and before final approval, the refund is [amount or calculation based on defined deliverables or phases]. The work-start point is [specific project milestone].
- After final approval or launch, the refund is [amount, conditions, or applicable resolution process]. If an included deliverable is missing or fails to match the proposal, contact us through the workspace so we can review it and explain the available resolution.
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within [timeframe]. Your payment provider may take additional time to post the refund.

## Staff checklist

1. Review the submitted brief. Use **Next step** to name the person who acts next, give their action, and state when they should expect an update.
2. Before saving a complete proposal, confirm the deliverables, total price, preview schedule, included revisions, hosting, domain, care, and cancellation terms. A save with a complete proposal makes it available to the customer.
3. Update the project stage as work progresses. Post the preview, collect feedback, and record or verify final approval before coordinating launch.
4. For an unpaid revision, review any checkout revision alert in admin. For a paid proposal, preserve the accepted terms and handle additional scope separately.
